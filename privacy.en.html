Clauses of material importance to you are highlighted in bold.

2.3 Information processed through system services

  • Voice search: When you tap the microphone and speak, audio is sent to Apple's speech-recognition service for transcription (see sections 3.4 and 5.1). We do not keep recordings.
  • Map tiles: When you view an outdoor running route, map tiles and necessary region data are provided by Apple Maps (iOS) or AMap (Android); see section 5.
  • In-app purchases: Lifetime membership transactions are handled by Apple StoreKit; Apple may receive device and account information to complete the transaction. We receive only the transaction receipt and purchase status — never card numbers or billing addresses.
  • Health data interchange: With your authorization, the App may read/write steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, nutrition, water and workouts via Apple HealthKit / Android Health Connect. Exchange happens inside the system health framework (see section 5.2).

2.4 Information we do NOT collect

The App does not do any of the following:

  • Collect the device advertising identifier (IDFA), perform ad tracking, or serve advertisements;
  • Integrate third-party analytics SDKs (no behavioral tracking, no usage profiles);
  • Collect location in the background — location is used in the foreground only while you actively run or walk outdoors;
  • Read contacts, SMS, call history, or the clipboard (we read a template share code from the clipboard only when you actively tap "Paste");
  • Fetch remote configuration or use crash-reporting services (these capabilities are disabled in this release; see section 5.5).

3. Sensitive Personal Information

Under the PIPL, health/medical data, biometric information, location tracks, and information of minors under 14 are sensitive personal information. Sensitive processing in the App is as follows:

  • Health and physiological data: weight, body fat, circumferences, diet, menstrual cycle, sleep, heart rate, etc. — stored locally and encrypted only, used for your own self-managed health care, never for advertising, profiling, or provision to third parties;
  • Location tracks: foreground GPS only while you actively run outdoors; collection stops when exercise ends; routes stay on-device;
  • Biometrics: Face ID / fingerprint invoke only the system's local authentication for sensitive operations; the system returns only "success/failure". We cannot read and do not store your face or fingerprint raw templates;
  • Voice: transcribed by Apple servers; we retain no audio.

All processing of the above is based on your separate consent (obtained through the first-launch privacy prompt and the system permission dialog at first use of each feature). Declining a permission does not affect features unrelated to that permission.

5.1 How speech recognition works

  • Audio capture starts only after you actively tap the microphone button;
  • Audio is sent via Apple's Speech framework to Apple servers for transcription (on supported regions/OS versions, some recognition may happen on-device, at the system's discretion);
  • We save neither recordings nor identifiable recognition records; transcribed text is used only for the current food search;
  • You can disable microphone or speech-recognition permission at any time in system settings.

5.2 Health data

HealthKit / Health Connect data is used strictly within the read/write scope you authorized. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used for profiling, and never shared with third parties (other than the system health platforms themselves). The App does not read health data proactively in the background.

5.3 Map services

Maps load only on pages where you actively view a running route. The AMap SDK initializes only after you accept this policy, per its compliance requirements. We do not provide your diet or workout records to the map provider.

5.4 Payments

The App sells only a one-time, non-consumable "Lifetime" membership; there are no auto-renewable subscriptions. Purchases, charges, and refunds are handled by Apple under App Store terms.

5.5 Integrated capabilities currently disabled

To allow smooth future use of cloud backup, cross-device sync, and stability improvements, the App retains code for cloud sync (Tencent CloudBase), crash reporting (Sentry), and remote configuration. However, in this release, without configured service credentials, these capabilities do not initialize, make no network requests, and collect no information. If ever enabled, we will update this policy, list providers/fields/purposes item by item, and obtain your consent again.

7. Storage, Security and Retention

7.1 Location and method of storage

  • All user data is stored in an encrypted local database (Hive, AES-256) on your device;
  • The encryption key is randomly generated on-device and kept in iOS Keychain / Android Keystore with the attribute "this device only, accessible when unlocked"; it does not migrate via iCloud/Google backup;
  • Progress photos, avatars, and media directories are excluded from system cloud backups;
  • When the App enters background, iOS masks the snapshot and Android sets FLAG_SECURE, preventing sensitive content leakage in the app switcher.

7.2 Retention period

Data remains local while you use the App. It is deleted and unrecoverable when: (1) you uninstall the App; (2) you run "Clear all data" under "Profile › Data Management"; (3) you delete an individual record. Because storage is local and encrypted, we hold no copy of your data and cannot restore it after deletion; please use the export feature for your own backups.

7.3 Limits of security measures

We apply encrypted storage, local key isolation, biometric verification, backup exclusion, and jailbreak/root risk warnings, but no internet or electronic storage is absolutely secure. If the device is stolen, jailbroken/rooted, or unlocked by someone else, local data may still be accessed directly. Please set a device passcode and keep the device safe.

9. Your Rights

Under Articles 44–50 of the PIPL, you have the rights below. Since all data resides on your own device with no account system, most rights can be exercised instantly in the App:

  • Right to know and decide: via this policy and the first-launch prompt, and by granting or denying each permission;
  • Access and copy: view all records on feature pages; "Profile › Data Management › Export" outputs all data as JSON/CSV (portability);
  • Correct and supplement: edit any record or body parameter at any time;
  • Erasure: delete individual records or clear all data in one tap;
  • Withdraw consent: disable any permission in system settings, or withdraw agreement to this policy under "Profile › Data & Privacy" (the App returns to the agreement page and stops the related processing); withdrawal does not affect prior consent-based processing;
  • Right to explanation: request an explanation of processing rules via the section 13 channels;
  • Account deletion: the App has no accounts, so no cancellation is needed; uninstalling or clearing data is equivalent;
  • Next-of-kin rights: close relatives may contact us via section 13 regarding posthumous personal-information interests based on lawful and legitimate grounds.

We will respond within 15 working days after receiving and verifying your request.

11. Automated Calculation and Algorithm Notes

Calorie, nutrient-target, and training-volume results are computed locally in real time from publicly available general formulas (e.g., the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR equation, metabolic equivalent tables) and the parameters you enter. This is not "automated decision-making" under the PIPL: the results do not determine any transaction, pricing, or differential treatment. You can change the inputs at any time or ignore the suggestions. Detailed methodology is available on the in-app plan explanation pages or on request.
